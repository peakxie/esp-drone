ESP-DRONE SLOW THROTTLE RAMP-UP ISSUE - QUICK REFERENCE
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Drone slides sideways instead of lifting during slow throttle ramp-up

ROOT CAUSE: Combination of motor clipping + integral windup

================================================================================
5. SEQUENCE OF EVENTS DURING SLOW RAMP-UP
================================================================================

Phase 1: Throttle Ramp (drone on ground, thrust < weight)
  - User slowly increases throttle: 0 → 50 → 100 → 150 → 200
  - Drone has slight attitude error (e.g., tilted right)
  - Rate PID integral accumulates: integ += error × dt
  - By time thrust reaches 200:
    * Integral is close to saturation (±33.3)
    * Rate PID output = 440 × 33.3 ≈ 14,652 correction needed

Phase 2: Liftoff (thrust crosses weight threshold)
  - Drone starts lifting with thrust ≈ 230
  - Motor mixing with saturated PID:
    * M1 = 230 - 14,652 = -14,422 → limitThrust() clips to 0
    * M2 = 230 + 14,652 = 14,882 → limitThrust() clips to UINT16_MAX
    * M3 = 230 - 14,652 = -14,422 → clips to 0
    * M4 = 230 + 14,652 = 14,882 → clips to UINT16_MAX

Phase 3: Motor Asymmetry
  - Motors 1&3 off (clipped to 0)
  - Motors 2&4 maxed out
  - Result: Extreme roll moment
  - Drone slides sideways instead of lifting

Why fast throttle works: Less time for integral to accumulate
